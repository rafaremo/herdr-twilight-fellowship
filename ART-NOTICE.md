# Art notice

All images in this repo were generated with OpenAI image models through OpenCode, then keyed, cut, resized and animated by `scripts/build-art.py`:

- `mods/twilight-fellowship-agents/party-sprites/*.gif` and `party-art/*.png`: the sixteen characters. Each comes from one generated four-pose sheet, shrunk to 36×48 pixels and shown at 4×. The animations are frames of that sheet; the breathing and the `!` are added in code.
- `mods/twilight-fellowship-spaces/art/*.png`, `mods/twilight-fellowship-tabs/art/*.png`: buildings and panel icons.
- `mods/twilight-fellowship-chrome/fellowship-sidebar.png`, `fellowship-map.png`: backgrounds, tinted toward the theme's blue.
- `preview.png`, `layouts/twilight-fellowship.png`: composed from the images above. They are not screenshots.
